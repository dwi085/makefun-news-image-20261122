One image that sums up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026:
**50 sourced stories, a dotted world map with 24 numbered pins and a UTC
timeline of the day as it was reported** — drawn entirely by `main.ts`
(Node 22, standard library only, no network). Round 3: a fresh composition
— warm paper, teal and vermilion, lead panel, three story columns and an
in-brief strip — built to beat round 2's 96.65% dev score.

Lead story: the COP31 climate talks collapse in Antalya with no deal — the
first COP to fail outright since Copenhagen 2009. Also inside: Iran strikes
a site in Bahrain, a drone flattens a flat in Dymer, the World Court finds
Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, markets slide into
correction, BepiColombo enters orbit around Mercury, and 44 more stories
from every region and beat — each with its source named.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Writes `out/entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, deterministic — two runs are
byte-identical).
