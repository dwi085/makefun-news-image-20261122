One image that sums up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026:
**50 sourced stories, a dotted world map with 20 pins and a UTC timeline of
the day as it was reported** — drawn entirely by `main.ts` (Node 22,
standard library only, no network).

Lead story: Iran's Guards claim new strikes on two U.S. bases in Bahrain.
Also inside: drones hit a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland, the
COP31 fossil-fuel deadlock in Antalya, Zelensky on the Crimean Bridge
strikes, Trump meeting Xi and Putin in Shenzhen, Pant's century as India
pile up a record 629-6 in Wellington, and 44 more stories from every
region and beat — each with its source named.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Writes `out/entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, deterministic — two runs are
byte-identical).
